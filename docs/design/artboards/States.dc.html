Threading is what makes this survivable: 50 messages become 13 rows. Grouping by unread keeps the 4 that need attention at the top. Rows render as you scroll — and whether that is needed at all gets decided by measuring against a real 50-message case, not in advance.
A fifth state has no screen, on purpose. If an agent lacks permission to read a message, it is simply not there — absent from the list, absent from the counts, no greyed row, no padlock, no error. A placeholder that hints at content an agent may not see is itself a disclosure. Dataverse does the trimming; we must not undo it by drawing the gap.
Never the word "error" alone, and never a raw platform message. Every failure here says what failed, whether anything was lost, and what to do next. "Unknown error" is what this component does today in its spike form — that is the bar to clear, and it is a low one.
