The two languages differ on exactly three things: Fluent/D365 — 4px radii, hairline rules, no elevation, rows 30px, flush white surfaces. New Outlook — 8px radii, pill controls, shadow2 cards on a #f5f5f5 canvas, rows 56px, 32px avatars. Same colour tokens in both. The choice is density and surface, not palette.
